Drop photos in this folder (src/photos) with these exact names. They appear on the site
automatically, resized and compressed for phones. Until a photo is here, its spot shows
a soft placeholder.

richa.jpg       your main photo (portrait, about 800 x 1000 px or bigger)
thor.jpg        Thor (landscape)
dog-1.jpg       any dog you love (square)
404-dog.jpg     a cute doggy photo for the "page not found" page (square)
lens/           your nature photos for the Photos page, named 1.jpg, 2.jpg, 3.jpg ...
                Add as many as you like; they show in number order.
                1.jpg and 2.jpg also appear on the home page.

.jpg, .png and .webp all work. Big photos are fine; the site shrinks them for you.
