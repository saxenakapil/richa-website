About me

A listener first.

I was raised in Agra, a small town where neighbours look out for each other and helping someone is just what you do. I've lived in quite a few big cities since, but I still value people over polish.

I love meeting different people and hearing their stories. Somewhere in those stories, I found a problem I knew I could solve. (More on that below.)

Off the clock, I'm mom to a wonderful, awesome boy, a fiction lover (Harry Potter forever ), and someone who truly believes the best things in life are food and sleep. Everything else is a bonus.

The journey

From the classroom to the boardroom.
(same heart, bigger rooms)

  1. 2003 - 2018
    🍎 The classroom

    Educator, then Academic Coordinator & Internal Auditor

    15 years across six K-12 schools in Agra, Greater Noida and Chennai. I mentored a team of 24 teachers, ran training workshops, welcomed new faculty, and kept quality high through audits.

    2,000+ students later, some are in college now, and they still reach out to say I believed in them. That never gets old.

  2. 2019 - now
    🚀 Uniify

    Co-founder & CHRO

    I collected people's stories, and in them I found a problem worth solving: engagement, wellbeing and internal comms are deeply connected, yet most organisations manage them in silos. So we built Uniify to bring them together, so HR teams could see culture, act on it, and prove its impact.

    I owned the full employee journey for our own teams, from hiring and onboarding to engagement, performance and exits, while advising HR leaders at 50+ organisations across industries and countries.

    Our first client came from 3,000 km away, in an industry we weren't sure about.

    Two wonderful women in Georgia took Uniify for a 2-month pilot. They worked in the casino space and doubted it would fit. One month in, they came back asking for a full subscription. Peer recognition went up 121% in a quarter.

    It worked where even we weren't sure it would. That's when we knew.

  3. 2023 - now
    🤝 HRIN

    Founding Member & Community Admin

    Helped shape HRIN, a closed, vetted global community for HR professionals, from its early days. Today I keep it a trusted space by vetting members and curating conversations.

  4. 2025 - now
    ⚖️ Chitkara University

    Jury Member

    I sit on startup screening and selection committees at the Chitkara Innovation Incubation Foundation for the MeitY GENESIS EiR Program and the Startup India Seed Fund Scheme.

Studied biology, then English, then HR. Turns out people were always the subject.

What I believe

Three things I'd say in any room.

01

People are more than they seem.

In good ways and not-so-good ways. That's exactly why kindness and empathy always help.

02

Kindness doesn't check status.

My father spoke to everyone the same way, whoever they were: kindly. I grew up thinking that's simply how you talk to people. Everyone makes mistakes, they're human. So before I judge, I stand in their shoes.

03

Culture lives in small moments.

Not in celebrations, gifts and vouchers. In a peer's small gesture, a colleague's helping hand, a leader's fair decision, or someone standing by you when you need it most. The everyday things make a long journey together possible.

"In a world where you can be anything, be kind."

It's how I lead, and always will. Learned it from my dad. 💜

Different rooms, same person

  • Students called me kind. (even when I was strict)
  • Teammates called me reachable.
  • Clients called me helpful.
